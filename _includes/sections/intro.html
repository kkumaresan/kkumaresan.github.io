  • Cloud & platform — Azure and AWS, Kubernetes, microservices, event-driven integration
  • Business & value — cloud economics, cost governance, customer discovery translated into architecture
  • AI enablement — enterprise-safe LLM tooling, RAG and vector search, agents, AI governance

I work at the system level — not the feature level.
