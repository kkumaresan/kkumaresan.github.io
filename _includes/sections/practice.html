{% include icon.html name="migrate" %}Legacy Modernization & Cloud Migration

When cloud costs rise and delivery velocity slows, architecture is usually the root cause. I run modernization end to end — from the business case that gets it funded through to the services in production.

  • Build the re-architecture proposal and carry it through stakeholder approval
  • Define target-state architecture and the sequencing to reach it
  • Decompose monoliths into microservices ecosystems without stalling the roadmap
  • Design Kubernetes-native CI/CD automation frameworks
  • Improve observability, scalability, resilience, and compliance posture

At NielsenIQ this meant a 15-year monolith re-architected and migrated to Azure in eight months, with the first new services live in six — and CI/CD driven to 100% platform coverage on containerized Kubernetes pipelines, migrating Jenkins to GitHub Actions.

{% include icon.html name="economics" %}Cloud Economics & Cost Governance

Infrastructure spend is an architecture output, not a procurement problem. I treat it as a design constraint with a business case attached.

  • Model cloud economics and build the case for structural change
  • Establish FinOps and cloud cost governance operating models
  • Right-size through re-architecture rather than through deferral
  • Hold the savings with governance that survives the next roadmap cycle

Executed in two phases at NielsenIQ: on-prem to cloud removed 20% of infrastructure cost; a subsequent VM-to-AKS migration removed a further 27% — roughly 47% in total over two years.
